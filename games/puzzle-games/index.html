How to Play Sudoku

  • Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with digits 1–9
  • Each digit may appear only once per row, column and box
  • Click a cell, then press a number key or tap the numpad
  • Arrow keys navigate between cells; Delete/Backspace clears
  • Pencil mode writes small candidate marks instead of values
  • Hints (3 per game) reveal one correct empty cell

How to Play 15-Puzzle

  • Arrange tiles 1–15 in order, left-to-right, top-to-bottom
  • Click a tile next to the empty space to slide it over
  • Arrow keys also move the empty space (moves adjacent tile)
  • Try to solve it in the fewest moves possible!
  • Best score is the fewest moves saved across sessions

How to Play Memory Match

  • Flip two cards per turn to find matching pairs of emojis
  • Matched pairs stay face-up; unmatched cards flip back over
  • Clear the board by matching all pairs to win!
  • Tab to focus a card, then press Enter or Space to flip it
  • Best time per difficulty is saved between sessions
