♟ Chess Rules

  • Move pieces by clicking a piece then clicking the destination (green dots).
  • Red-highlighted squares are capture moves.
  • Pawn: moves forward 1 square (2 from start), captures diagonally. En passant supported.
  • Knight: L-shape (2+1). Jumps over other pieces.
  • Bishop: any number of diagonal squares.
  • Rook: any number of horizontal/vertical squares.
  • Queen: bishop + rook combined.
  • King: one square any direction. Castling supported.
  • You cannot make a move that leaves your king in check.
  • Checkmate (king in check, no legal moves) ends the game.
  • Pawn reaching the back rank auto-promotes — choose your piece!

⚪ Checkers / Draughts Rules

  • Red moves from bottom, White from top. Pieces move diagonally forward on dark squares.
  • Click a piece, then click a green dot to move it.
  • If a jump (capture) is available, you must take it (mandatory capture).
  • Capture by jumping over an opponent's piece to the empty square behind it.
  • Multiple consecutive jumps in one turn are allowed if available.
  • When a piece reaches the opposite end, it becomes a King (shown with ♛) and can move/capture in all diagonal directions.
  • Win by capturing all opponent pieces or leaving them with no legal moves.
